BrandForge v1.7.41

What changed
- Slider Line Up single-variant centering logic was corrected.
- Multi-item Line Up rows still center the whole visible product group across the 600x600 canvas.
- Single-item Line Up rows no longer get pulled into the middle of the whole canvas.
- Instead, a single visible product now stays in its original slot and is optically balanced inside that slot using the product's actual visible bounds.
- This keeps top/bottom and left/right whitespace close enough while preserving the intended placement.

Files changed
- src/modules/slider/jsx/sliderEngine.jsx
- test/sliderLineupCentering.js
- package.json
- package-lock.json

Notes
- No crop or resize rules were changed.
- Captions and shadows still move together with the Line Up group.
